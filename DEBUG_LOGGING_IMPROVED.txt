POSSIBLE ERRORS WE MIGHT SEE:
══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

• "User rejected the request" 
  → You clicked "Cancel" in wallet

• "Insufficient funds for gas" 
  → Not enough BNB for gas fee

• "Provider not available" 
  → Wallet extension issue

• "Chain switch failed" 
  → Wallet didn't switch to BSC

• "eth_sendTransaction" errors
  → RPC or wallet communication issue

• "Internal JSON-RPC error"
  → BSC RPC issue or wallet issue
